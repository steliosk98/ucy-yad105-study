# Greek terminology glossary — YAD105

Convention for the Greek side of the bank: **write the prose in proper Greek, give the
formal Greek term first, and put the English in parentheses on first use within that
answer.** Each question is read standalone as a flashcard, so "first use" resets per entry.

Not everything gets a gloss. Identifiers keep their exact form: SQL keywords (`SELECT`,
`DISTINCT`, `COMMIT`), product and technology names (Oracle, APEX, REST, JSON, HTTP),
and code fragments. Glossing those would be noise.
